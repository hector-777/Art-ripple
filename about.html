我想做一件什麼樣的東西

What I'm Trying to Make

我想像中的畫面是這樣的——

你走進一個完全黑暗的房間。腳下是地板，但你看不到邊界。

周圍垂落著許多透明的軟管，裡面注滿了水。光從你頭頂的位置打下來，只照亮你所在的那一小片區域。你往前走，光跟著你走，新的水管從黑暗中浮現；你回頭，身後的水管重新隱入黑暗。

你伸手碰了一根水管。它晃了一下，裡面的水開始波動，光影跟著晃動，像漣漪一樣擴散開來。水管末端掛著的小鈴鐺或貝殼，發出輕輕的碰撞聲——那是物理的聲音，不是喇叭播出來的。

你看不清全貌。光只追隨你。每一步都是在探索。

安靜，緩慢，有一點孤獨感，但光始終陪著你。

This is the picture in my head——

You walk into a completely dark room. Floor beneath you, but you can't see the edges.

Transparent hoses hang from the ceiling, filled with water. Light comes from above your head, illuminating only the small area where you stand. You move forward, the light follows — new hoses emerge from the darkness. You look back, the ones you passed fade away.

You reach out and touch a hose. It sways. The water inside moves, light ripples across the space. A small bell or shell at the end of the hose makes a soft, physical sound — not from a speaker.

You can't see the whole thing. The light only follows you. Every step is an exploration.

Quiet, slow, a little lonely — but the light is always with you.

材質的選擇

Choosing the Material

材質這件事，我想了很久。

我一開始想用細線，因為夠輕、夠隱形。但後來發現，細線在光裡幾乎看不見。我要的是「有體積感的東西」——它必須在黑暗裡存在，但又不是一個實心的柱子擋在你面前。

後來我試了透明水管，裡面灌水。

光穿過水的時候，會折射、會散射。整根管子會微微發光，像一根透明的光柱。當你碰到它，水在裡面晃動——那個晃動是物理的、有機的、不可預測的。每一次晃動都不一樣。這是任何電腦特效都做不出來的。

水管末端掛上小鈴鐺或貝殼，水的晃動會帶動它們發出輕響。那是真實的物理碰撞，不是喇叭。

I spent a long time thinking about material.

I started with thin threads — light, invisible. But then I realized: you can barely see them in the dark. I wanted something with volume — something that exists in the darkness, but isn't a solid pillar blocking your way.

Then I tried transparent hoses. Filled with water.

Light passing through water refracts and scatters. The whole hose glows slightly, like a translucent column of light. When you touch it, the water moves — and that movement is physical, organic, unpredictable. Every ripple is different. No computer-generated effect can replicate it.

Small bells or shells hang at the ends. The swaying water moves them, creating real, physical sounds. Not from speakers.

光的邏輯，與你的身體

Light & Your Body

房間是全黑的。沒有環境光，沒有「底光」。

光只來自於你頭頂的那一束——它會跟著你移動。你走到哪，光就跟到哪。所以你看不到整個空間的全貌，只能看到你所在位置的周圍。

你碰水管的時候，它會晃。水的晃動帶動光影波動，漣漪就出現了。風鈴的響聲是隨機的、物理的、每一次都不太一樣。你繼續往前走，新的水管從黑暗中浮現，身後的水管重新消失。

一切都很慢。一切都需要你的身體來觸發。

The room is completely dark. No ambient light, no "base light."

Light comes only from the beam above you — and it follows you. Wherever you go, the light goes. You can't see the whole space, only the area around you.

When you touch a hose, it sways. The movement of water shifts the light — ripples appear. The wind chimes sound randomly, physically, slightly different each time. You move forward, new hoses emerge from darkness, and the ones behind you fade back in.

Everything is slow. Everything needs your body to trigger it.

為什麼先做這個網頁？

Why Build a Website First?

實體裝置要做起來，牽涉到很多工程問題——水循環、水管密封、人體追蹤、投影對位⋯⋯每一項都需要時間和資源去試。

在還沒辦法直接做實體之前，我決定先用程式碼做一個數位原型。也就是你看到的第一頁。

它有幾個目的：

・ 驗證概念 — 這個互動邏輯真的成立嗎？光隨人動的感覺對不對？
・ 快速迭代 — 改程式比改實體快得多，我可以一直調到感覺對了為止。
・ 拿來溝通 — 讓其他人（包括你）可以實際體驗，而不是只看設計圖。

所以這個網站不是「作品的替代品」，它是這個嘗試的草稿、測試場、溝通工具。

Building the physical installation involves a lot of engineering — water circulation, hose sealing, body tracking, projection mapping. Each takes time and resources to test.

Before I could build the real thing, I decided to make a digital prototype with code. That's the first page you see.

It serves a few purposes:

・ Validate the concept — Does this interaction actually work? Does "light follows person" feel right?
・ Iterate fast — Changing code is faster than changing materials. I can tweak until it feels right.
・ Communicate — Let others (including you) actually experience it, not just look at sketches.

So this website isn't a replacement for the installation. It's a sketch, a testing ground, a communication tool.

這個網頁做了什麼

What This Web Page Does

第一頁（index.html）做了幾件事：

我用 150 個隨機散布的節點來模擬水管的叢林。滑鼠就是那一束光——你移動滑鼠，光就移動。靠近節點的時候，節點會被輕輕推開，然後產生漣漪。

漣漪不是完美的圓形。它會跟著滑鼠移動的方向拉伸，像水波被吹往同一個方向。漣漪也會在節點之間擴散，一層一層衰減下去。節點被推開之後，會慢慢地擺回來——像真實的水管被碰到之後的晃動。

即使你什麼都不做，每隔十幾秒也會有 1-2 個節點自動泛起漣漪。因為這個空間應該是活的，不是死寂的。

What the first page (index.html) does:

I used 150 randomly scattered nodes to simulate the hose jungle. The mouse is that beam of light — you move it, the light moves. When you get close to a node, it gets pushed slightly, and a ripple spreads.

The ripples aren't perfect circles. They stretch in the direction you move the mouse, like water waves being blown in one direction. Ripples also spread between nodes, fading layer by layer. After being pushed, the node slowly swings back — like a real hose swaying after being touched.

Even if you do nothing, every ten seconds or so, 1-2 nodes automatically ripple. This space should feel alive, not dead.

關於暗色和亮色

On Dark and Light

一開始我做了暗色版本。朋友看了說：「好像星座喔。」

我當下覺得不太對。我要的不是星空，是漣漪啊。但我也理解為什麼他們會這樣想——深色背景上的發光點，人眼天生會把它們看成星星。

可是裝置本身其實有兩種狀態：關燈的時候，你看到的是光穿過水產生的發光漣漪；開燈的時候，你看到的是水晃動時投下的影子漣漪。

所以我在網頁裡也做了兩個模式：

I made the dark version first. Friends looked at it and said: "It's like a constellation."

I felt something was off. I wasn't aiming for a starry sky — I wanted ripples. But I also understand why they said that — human eyes naturally see glowing dots on a dark background as stars.

But the installation itself has two states. When the lights are off, you see luminous ripples from light passing through water. When the lights are on, you see shadow ripples cast by the moving water.

So I built two modes into the web page:
