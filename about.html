光 · 漣漪

沉浸式視聽互動裝置

Ripple

An Immersive Audiovisual Interactive Installation

作品概述

Overview

在完全黑暗的空間中，觀眾走入一片由透明注水軟管組成的「叢林」。頭頂的光束跟隨觀眾移動，照亮周圍。當觀眾觸碰軟管時，管內水晃動，光影產生漣漪，風鈴發出輕響。

你看不清全貌，只能一步步探索。光永遠只追隨自己所在的位置。

In a completely dark space, visitors step into a "jungle" of translucent water-filled hoses. A beam of light follows their movement, illuminating the surroundings. When they touch a hose, the water inside ripples, light dances across the space, and wind chimes sound softly.

You cannot see the whole picture — only what the light reveals. Step by step, you explore. The light follows only where you are.

空間 · 材質 · 光

Space · Material · Light

空間：全黑的房間。沒有環境光。觀眾進入時只能看到自己周圍被照亮的一小片區域。

材質：透明軟水管從天花板垂落，管內注滿清水。水管末端懸掛輕質發聲物（小鈴鐺、貝殼、竹片）。

為什麼選擇透明水管＋水？

・ 水管有體積感，視覺上比細線更「存在」
・ 注水後，光穿過水管會產生折射與散射，形成發光透明柱體
・ 觸碰時，管內水的晃動會產生極其有機、緩慢的光影變化——這是任何電子特效都無法模擬的
・ 水管的自然晃動會帶動末端發聲物，產生物理的、隨機的風鈴聲響

光的邏輯：

・ 完全黑暗 → 投影儀／追光燈的光束跟隨觀眾位置移動
・ 光隨人動 → 觀眾走到哪裡，光就跟到哪裡
・ 探索感 → 往前走，新的水管從黑暗中浮現；身後，剛經過的水管重新隱入黑暗

Space: A completely dark room. No ambient light. Visitors can only see the small area illuminated around them.

Material: Translucent soft hoses hanging from the ceiling, filled with clear water. Lightweight sounding objects (small bells, shells, bamboo pieces) are attached to the ends.

Why transparent hoses + water?

・ The hoses have volume — they feel more "present" than thin lines
・ When filled with water, light passing through them refracts and scatters, creating luminous translucent columns
・ When touched, the water inside moves, producing organic, slow light shifts that no electronic effect can replicate
・ The natural swaying of the hoses moves the sounding objects, creating physical, random wind chime sounds

Logic of Light:

・ Complete darkness → a projector or spotlight beam follows the visitor's position
・ Light follows the person → wherever you go, the light goes
・ Sense of exploration → new hoses emerge from darkness as you move forward; behind you, the ones you passed fade back into shadow

網頁原型 · 第一頁做了什麼

Web Prototype · What the First Page Does

為什麼先做網站？

實體裝置的落地涉及大量工程問題——水循環系統、水管密封、人體追蹤校準、投影對位、空間搭建。在時間有限的情況下，我們先做了一個數位交互原型，用程式碼模擬裝置的核心體驗。

Why build a website first?

Realizing the physical installation involves complex engineering — water circulation, hose sealing, body tracking calibration, projection mapping, space construction. With limited time, we first built a digital interactive prototype, using code to simulate the core experience of the installation.

第一頁（index.html）的核心機制：

  • 150 個節點隨機散佈，模擬「水管叢林」
  • 滑鼠即光束，光隨人動
  • 滑鼠靠近節點 → 節點被推開（~6px）+ 產生漣漪
  • 漣漪不規則、方向性拉伸（隨滑鼠移動方向）
  • 漣漪在節點間傳播（次級漣漪，逐級衰減）
  • 節點緩慢回擺（阻尼振盪，模擬物理回彈）
  • 自動漣漪（10-15秒隨機觸發，空間的生命感）
  • 環境音（極低頻 drone + 隨機風鈴）

What the first page (index.html) does:

  • 150 randomly scattered nodes, simulating the "hose jungle"
  • Mouse as beam of light — light follows the person
  • Mouse approaches a node → node is pushed (~6px) + generates a ripple
  • Ripples are irregular and stretch directionally (following mouse movement)
  • Ripples propagate between nodes (secondary ripples, fading step by step)
  • Nodes slowly swing back (damped oscillation, simulating physical rebound)
  • Auto-ripples (triggered randomly every 10-15 seconds, giving the space a sense of life)
  • Ambient sound (extremely low-frequency drone + random wind chime)

明暗雙生模式

Light / Shadow Dual Mode

現實中的裝置空間，本身就具備兩種觀看狀態：開燈與關燈。

・ 關燈時，空間全黑，投影儀的光穿過透明水管與水，產生發光的漣漪——這是「光之漣漪」。

・ 開燈時，環境光打在水管與水面上，水晃動時投下晃動的影子——這是「影之漣漪」。

兩種狀態，說的是同一件事：水在動，漣漪在擴散，光與影都在訴說觸碰的回應。

於是我們在網頁中也延續了這種「一體兩面」的設計——暗色模式與亮色模式，如同裝置中的關燈與開燈。切換之間，你看到的是同一個作品在兩種光照條件下的不同面貌。

The physical installation itself has two ways of being seen: lights off and lights on.

・ Lights off: the space is completely dark. Light from the projector passes through the transparent hoses and water, creating luminous ripples — this is the "Light Ripple."

・ Lights on: ambient light hits the hoses and water. As the water moves, it casts shifting shadows — this is the "Shadow Ripple."

Two states, one story: water moves, ripples spread, and both light and shadow speak of the response to touch.

We carried this "two sides of the same whole" into the web prototype — Dark Mode and Light Mode mirror the installation's lights-off and lights-on states. Toggle between them, and you see the same work in two different lights.
